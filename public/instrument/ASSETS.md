## Mechanical sound recordings

Thomas Lai's Mechanical Keyboard Simulator: https://github.com/tplai/kbsim
Recordings of NovelKeys Cream, Holy Panda and Kailh Box Navy mechanisms, with
separate press and release recordings. Distributed under MIT; the complete
copyright notice and license are in kbsim-LICENSE.txt.

Original media is retained. Runtime processing removes leading silence, fades
sample ends, balances levels, and shortens detent sounds. No remote sound or
texture service is needed at runtime.

Exact source URLs are recorded in sources.json.
